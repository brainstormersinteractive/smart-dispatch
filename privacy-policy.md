# Privacy Policy for Smart Dispatch
Last updated: Nov 15, 2026

Smart Dispatch ("the app") is developed by Brainstormers Interactive. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

### Information we collect
The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Any data you enter stays on your device.

### Permissions
The app requests Wi-Fi permission so it can access your Wi-Fi connection. This is used only for the app's features on your device, and no network information is collected or shared.

### Changes
We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

### Contact
If you have any questions, contact us at info@brainstormers.lk.
